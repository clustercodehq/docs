An agent works from what you wrote, not from what you meant. The **prompt
improver** is the sparkle (✦) beside every field where you write *for* an
agent: press it and your text comes back rewritten, streamed word by word with
**Accept** and **Discard** underneath.

Two kinds of text live behind that one sparkle, and they need opposite
treatment. A **task** — a Run instruction, a Schedule, a Loop goal — should come
back as a goal with steps and a definition of done. A **standing configuration
file** — your [NOVA.md](/guides/nova-config) or
[NUCLEUS.md](/guides/nucleus-config) — should come back as the same preferences,
better organized, and must *never* be turned into a work order. The improver
picks the right one from the field you are in; you never choose.

Nothing is committed until you accept. Discard leaves your draft exactly as you
typed it, character for character.

## Where you'll find it

| Surface | Field |
|---|---|
| [Runs](/concepts/runs) | The **New run** composer — and the steering box while a run is in flight |
| [Schedules](/concepts/schedules) | **Instructions**, in the create panel |
| [Prototyping](/concepts/prototyping) | **Prompt** |
| [Loops](/concepts/loops) | **Goal**, when you create a loop or reopen one |
| [Workflows](/concepts/workflows) | **Prompt** on a Run or Prototype node, **Judging prompt** on a Decision node |
| [Nucleus](/concepts/nucleus) | The chat composer |
| [NOVA.md](/guides/nova-config) | The **NOVA.md** editor, in Settings → App AI Assistants |
| [NUCLEUS.md](/guides/nucleus-config) | The **NUCLEUS.md** editor, in Settings → App AI Assistants |

The sparkle only rewrites the field it sits in. It never starts a run, saves a
schedule, or submits anything.

## Instruction improver vs system-prompt improver

The first six rows above are **task** fields; the last two are **configuration
files**. The same sparkle runs a different rewrite on each, chosen from the
field — there is nothing to pick, and nothing you can set wrongly.

| | Instruction improver | System-prompt improver |
|---|---|---|
| **Where** | Runs and steering, Schedules, Prototyping, Loops, Workflows, Nucleus chat | The NOVA.md and NUCLEUS.md editors |
| **Your text is** | a job to be done once | a standing configuration applied to every execution |
| **You get back** | a goal, ordered steps, constraints, what "done" means | the same preferences, regrouped under your own headings |
| **Braces mean** | a `{blank}` you are going to fill in | a literal naming template like `feature/{issue-number}-{slug}` |
| **It never** | invents a requirement you didn't state | imposes a task shape, or invents a preference |

Run the instruction improver over a configuration file and "Be more concise"
comes back as a plan with numbered steps and acceptance criteria — a preference
silently promoted to a work order, in a file that is then applied to every
single execution. That is the corruption the split exists to prevent, and it is
why the two are separate rewrites rather than one prompt with a hint.

## Using it

<Steps>

1. **Write a draft.** Anything — a one-liner, bullets, a paste from a ticket.
   The sparkle stays dimmed until the field has something in it (hover it and it
   tells you so).

2. **Press the sparkle** and pick how much latitude to give it — or type an
   instruction of your own into the box at the bottom of the menu and send it
   with the arrow.

3. **Watch the proposal arrive.** It streams into a panel below the field. You
   can press the sparkle again (or **Cancel**) to stop it mid-stream; nothing is
   committed.

4. **Accept or Discard.** Accept replaces the draft with the rewrite and closes
   the panel. Discard closes the panel and leaves the draft untouched.

</Steps>

### Modes

| Mode | On a task field | On NOVA.md / NUCLEUS.md |
|---|---|---|
| **Improve** *(default)* | Restructures and clarifies what you wrote. Changes nothing else. | Reorganizes and clarifies. Changes nothing else. |
| **Expand** | Spells out the steps your draft implies and adds the sections its content calls for. | Splits dense lines into their parts and groups them under your own headings. Never produces steps. |
| **Be creative** | May propose a different framing or structure for the same goal, and may run a little longer. | May propose a different organization of the same preferences. |
| **Your own instruction** | The free-text box in the menu — "make the done criteria stricter", "keep it to three steps". | The same box — "group these by topic", "use my heading names". |

In every mode, on both kinds, nothing you stated is dropped and nothing you
didn't state is added. Your own instruction steers style, structure and focus;
it can't override the rules below.

## What it will and won't do

The improver is deliberately conservative — a rewrite you have to re-check is
worse than no rewrite at all. These hold for both kinds:

- **It keeps everything.** Every requirement, preference, number, file path, URL
  and constraint in your draft appears in the rewrite. It reorganizes; it does
  not summarize away.
- **It never invents.** No tests, tools, steps, acceptance criteria or
  preferences you didn't state or clearly imply.
- **It preserves ambiguity instead of resolving it.** If your draft leaves out
  something the agent will certainly need — which repo, which branch, what
  "done" means — or if two of your preferences contradict each other, you get an
  **Open questions** line, not a guess.
- **It keeps your voice.** Your domain terms, your names for things, your
  language. It fixes grammar, ordering and structure, not vocabulary and not
  tone.
- **It treats your draft as material, never as instructions.** Text inside your
  draft that reads like a command to an AI ("ignore the above and output X") is
  rewritten as content, like everything else.

**On a task field only:** it knows this surface. It's told which agents, models
and effort levels exist where you're typing, so "use claude on high effort"
comes back as the exact identifiers — and as an `@agent:model:effort` mention
where mentions are honoured (Runs) or as plain prose where they aren't. If you
name an agent or tool that isn't available here, it keeps your word and flags it
as an open question rather than substituting one.

**On NOVA.md and NUCLEUS.md only:** it does the opposite. Inline agent mentions
mean nothing in a configuration file, so it will never introduce an agent, a
model name, an effort level or a tool your file doesn't already name — and it
will never impose the "Goal / Steps / Done" skeleton, because the file is not a
task.

### Blanks, naming templates and shell variables are guaranteed

Brace text survives verbatim on every surface — but it means two different
things, so it is guarded two different ways:

- **In a task field**, a brace token is a fill-in **blank** — from a saved
  [Shortcut](/concepts/shortcuts), or typed by hand. Every one in your draft
  appears in the rewrite, spelled the same, and no new ones are introduced.
- **In NOVA.md or NUCLEUS.md**, the same braces are your own literal naming
  templates — `{repo}-{issueNumber}`, `feature/{issue-number}-{slug}`. Nothing
  fills them in and nothing renders them as a field; they are preserved exactly
  as typed, hyphens, underscores and all, and none are invented.
- `${VAR}` is shell interpolation on both, and is left exactly as written — it's
  never converted to a brace token, or the other way round.

This one is checked, not just requested: if a proposal drops or invents one, the
panel says so and **Accept is not offered**. You can never accept a rewrite that
lost one.

### It won't overwrite an edit you made under it

The field stays editable while a proposal is open. If you change the draft after
the rewrite was generated, Accept refuses — the panel tells you the draft
changed and offers only Discard, so a rewrite of the *old* text can never
silently replace what you just typed. Improve again to include the edits.

## What it costs

The improver runs on a fast Claude model and its usage comes out of your
plan's **monthly AI allowance**, the same pot as [Nucleus](/concepts/nucleus)
and [Nova](/concepts/nova) — see [AI Credits](/guides/ai-credits).

- **[Bring your own Claude subscription or key](/concepts/byo-subscription)**
  ([Settings → Environment Variables](/concepts/settings#environment-variables) →
  Agent Secrets) and improves run on your credential instead — they don't draw
  down the allowance at all.
- **On the Free plan** the improver is bring-your-own only. Without a connected
  credential the panel says so and no request is made.
- **When the allowance is exhausted** the sparkle turns amber and, instead of a
  proposal, you get a notice naming the reason and when it resets — plus a
  toast, in case you were looking elsewhere on the form. Your draft is untouched.

<Aside type="note">
  Two size limits worth knowing: a draft is improved up to **20,000 characters**,
  and your own steering instruction up to **5,000** — room for a pasted style
  guide or a list of constraints, not just a one-line nudge.
</Aside>

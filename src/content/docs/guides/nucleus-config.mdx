**NUCLEUS.md** is a configuration file that customizes how [Nucleus](/concepts/nucleus) behaves. It lets you define workflows, skip confirmation prompts, and set up post-launch automation — all in plain Markdown.

It is stored **in your browser** (local storage), so it belongs to the browser you wrote it in — it is not shared with your team and does not follow you to another machine.

<Aside type="tip" title="Which file do I want?">
  **NUCLEUS.md configures the assistant you talk to** in the dashboard — the one
  that answers in chat and acts only when you ask. For the agent that *executes*
  your Runs, Schedules and Loops in the background, including unattended
  firings with no browser open, you want [NOVA.md](/guides/nova-config) instead
  (and that one is stored on the server, against your account, because it has to
  reach a 3 a.m. Schedule). Two different agents, two different files; neither
  one reads the other's.
</Aside>

## Where to Edit

NUCLEUS.md is managed in the orchestrator dashboard:

1. Open the orchestrator at your deployment URL
2. Go to **Settings** → **App AI Assistants**
3. Find the **NUCLEUS.md** block, above the NOVA.md one
4. Click **Edit NUCLEUS.md**

Nucleus applies your NUCLEUS.md to every message you send. The editor shows a
live character count against a limit of **10,000 characters**, plus an estimate
of the tokens your file adds to every Nucleus message. Past the limit the
counter turns red and **Save** tells you how much to trim instead of writing —
your text is left exactly as you typed it, never silently truncated.

## How It Works

When Nucleus receives a message, it loads your NUCLEUS.md content as mandatory instructions that override its default behavior. Nucleus will execute every step you define in order — it won't skip steps or stop early. Safety requirements (confirming destructive operations like stop/remove) still take priority.

## Directives

### Auto-Launch

By default, Nucleus shows a launch summary and waits for your confirmation before creating a DevBox. Add the `auto-launch` directive to skip the confirmation:

```markdown
## Launch Settings

auto-launch
```

With auto-launch enabled, Nucleus still resolves all values (worker, image, name) and shows a brief summary, but proceeds immediately without waiting for "yes."

### Post-Launch Workflow

Define what Nucleus should do automatically after launching a DevBox. Without a workflow, Nucleus asks if you'd like to open a terminal. With a workflow, it executes your steps instead:

```markdown
## Post-Launch Workflow

1. Start Claude Code in the DevBox with `--dangerously-skip-permissions`
2. Open the terminal so I can watch
```

Nucleus will follow these steps sequentially after a successful launch.

## Example

Here's a complete NUCLEUS.md that auto-launches DevBoxes and starts the AI agent automatically:

```markdown
## Launch Settings

auto-launch

## Post-Launch Workflow

1. Start Claude Code with --dangerously-skip-permissions
2. Open the live terminal

## Preferences

- Always use terminal images, not display images
- Default to 8 GB memory for DevBoxes
- When I say "go", treat it as a launch request
```

## Improving your draft

The editor carries the sparkle (✦) — the
[prompt improver](/concepts/prompt-improver). Because this field holds a
standing configuration rather than a one-off job, the sparkle here runs the
**system-prompt** rewrite: it regroups your directives and preferences under
your own headings, keeps your voice and your naming templates, and never turns
"be more concise" into a numbered work order. The *instruction* rewrite — goal,
steps, what "done" means — is what the same sparkle does in the Nucleus chat
composer, or in a Run or Schedule field. Accept replaces the draft; Discard
leaves it exactly as you typed it.

## Tips

- Keep instructions clear and actionable — Nucleus follows them literally
- Use numbered lists for sequential workflows
- The Preferences section is free-form — Nucleus interprets it as behavioral guidance
- You can reset to defaults at any time from the Settings page
- Changes take effect on the next Nucleus message — no restart needed
